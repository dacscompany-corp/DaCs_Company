Overhead Expenses — Guide

What is Overhead Expenses?

This module tracks overhead in two scopes. Company overhead is the cost of running the business — office rent, electricity, internet, software. Project overhead is the cost of running a specific job: its site operating costs (fuel, temporary utilities, permits, equipment rental) plus its support people — coordinator, site supervision, procurement staff and fees.

Overhead / Indirect Labor is recorded in Expenses → Payroll (choose the Overhead / Indirect Labor category) and appears here automatically, marked Payroll. Don't re-enter those people here — it would count them twice. Overhead pay never draws down a worker's pakyaw contract.

Month Filter
Use the Month picker at the top to view expenses for any specific month. Only entries recorded in that month will appear.
Add Expense
Click + Add Expense to record a new overhead cost. Fill in the date, category (e.g. Water, Electricity), amount, and an optional description.
KPI Cards
The three cards at the top show Company Overhead, Project Overhead, and Overall Overhead for the selected month, each with its change against the previous month.
By Category
Expenses are automatically grouped by category so you can see which area costs the most. Click View Details on any category card to drill down into its entries.
Delete Entry
To remove an incorrect entry, click the red trash icon on the right side of any row in the Expense Records table.

Accomplishment Reports — Guide

What are Accomplishment Reports?

Accomplishment Reports summarize the work completed and costs incurred per project. Each report is linked to a project folder and can be shared with the client for review and approval.

Project Grid

Project Cards
Each card is one project folder. BOQ Saved means a report exists; No Report Yet means one needs to be created. Click Create / Open Report → to enter the editor.
Card Summary
Each card shows Total Project Cost (sum of all line items) and Total Accomplishment (billed amount based on % complete) for a quick progress overview.

Inside the Report Editor

Document Info
Fill in the Date, Project Name, Area (sqm), Owner Name, and Location. These fields appear in the printed / exported PDF header.
Terms & Conditions
Check I. Terms of Payment, II. Exclusions, and/or III. Duration to include those sections in the PDF. Edit the text inside each box to match the project's agreed terms.
Status Workflow
Use the Draft → Submit → Approve buttons in the top bar to advance the report stage. Draft = in progress, Submitted = sent to client, Approved = client confirmed.
Print / Export PDF
Click Print to open the browser print dialog (choose "Save as PDF") or Export PDF to download a formatted copy to share with the client. Always Save first before printing.
Save
Click Save (or press Ctrl+S) to store all changes. Unsaved changes are indicated by a warning when navigating away.

Project folders must be created in the Expenses module first before an Accomplishment Report can be built for them.

Payment Requests — Guide

What are Payment Requests?

Payment Requests are company-issued billing notices sent to clients. You can create, track, and manage all payment requests from this module.

New Payment Request
Click + New Payment Request to create a billing notice for a client. Fill in the client, project, billing period, amount, and due date.
Status Tracking
Each request has a status: Pending (awaiting client action), Approval Pending (client submitted proof), Submitted, or Verified / Paid. The stat cards at the top show counts per status.
Search & Filter
Use the search bar to find requests by client name, project, or billing period. Use the All Statuses dropdown to narrow down by a specific status.
SOA (Statement of Account)
Click SOA on any row to generate and view the Statement of Account for that billing request.
QR Settings
Use QR Settings to manage the QR codes attached to payment requests, so clients can scan to pay or view details easily.

Invoice Receipt — Guide

What is Invoice Receipt?

This module lets you create, manage, and track official invoice receipts issued to clients. All invoices are grouped by client for easy reference.

New Invoice
Click + New Invoice to create an invoice receipt for a client. Fill in the client details, items, amounts, and dates.
Invoice Listing
All invoices are grouped by client. Click a client row to expand and see each individual receipt with its invoice number, date, amount, TIN, and status.
KPI Cards
The four cards at the top show Total Invoices, Total Billed (lifetime), This Month billing amount, and total Issued finalized invoices at a glance.
Export CSV
Click Export CSV to download all invoice records as a spreadsheet file for reporting or accounting purposes.
Business Settings
Use Business Settings to configure your company's name, address, TIN, and other details that appear printed on every invoice.
