Our Story

Our journey began with a collective of architects and engineers driven by a singular purpose: to redefine modern living through sustainable design in apartments, residences, and condominiums.

At the heart of this movement is Architect Cristher Dacome, whose personal history of struggle and inspiration paved the way for this new era. He founded DACS not merely as a business, but as a declaration of faith — honoring God's unwavering faithfulness and recognizing the divine provision that continues to sustain this mission.

Ar. Cristher DacomeFounder, DAC's Building Design Services
