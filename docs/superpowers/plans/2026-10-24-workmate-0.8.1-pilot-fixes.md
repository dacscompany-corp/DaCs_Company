# WorkMate 0.8.1+1010 — 2A-4b pilot fixes F1–F3 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Fix the three findings of the 2A-4b phone pilot (24 Oct 2026) before launch: **F3** a refresh can spin for a minute or more after the signal comes back, so the buyer force-closes the app; **F1** a voided purchase shows "Voided" and a second "Void" tag; **F2** a purchase opened with no signal shows a bare "No signal" page that does not say a payment can still be recorded from the list.

**Architecture:** Phone only — no migration, no Dacs Web change. **F3:** every fresh read (My purchases, My cash, a purchase's payments) gets **one overall time limit** (20 s, every server call and every R38 round in it); after it the saved copy is shown, saying so. My purchases and its My cash card become **one refresh**: the three reads run **at the same time** inside one R38 read, so there is one "which of my sends went through?" check before and one after, not one pair per read, and both saved copies count the same sent payments. A late answer is thrown away: it writes no saved copy, marks nothing sent and changes nothing shown; a saved copy is only replaced by a read that began no earlier; and on every screen only the latest-started load may change what is shown. **F1/F2:** two small screen changes.

**Tech stack:** Flutter 3.38.9 / Dart 3.10.8, sqflite 2.4.2+1 — no dependency change.

**Based on:** [2A-4b plan, revision 3](2026-10-23-workmate-stage2a4b-buyer-payments.md) (R28–R39; its pilot P1–P14 + P4b passed on 0.8.0+1009, findings F1–F3). **R38 is kept whole:** sent payments stay until every saved copy counts them; a read during which a payment went is read again.

**Repo:** **`Documents/Dacs WorkMate`**, base **`752fa2b`** (Task 3 of 2A-4b, committed by the user 23 Oct). This plan file lives in Dacs Web with the other WorkMate plans.

**Status:** Revision 3, 24 October 2026, **for the user's review**. Revision 3 closes the review's payment-counting blocker (**R51**): when a purchase's payments could not be re-read, revision 2 kept the earlier balance but replaced the payments on the phone with ones counted against the saved list — two different snapshots, so "left to pay" and the next payment's suggested amount could be wrong. Now a failed payments read shows "needs signal" and no balance. Revision 2 closed the review's two F3 gaps: **R49** — My purchases and My cash show the **saved copy at once** and refresh behind it (revision 1 still waited for the network before showing it); **R50** — a purchase's screen asks for the purchase and its payments **at the same time under one time limit** (revision 1 asked one after the other, each with its own limit: about 15 + 20 s). F1, F2, R47 and R48 unchanged. ## Rulings

| # | Ruling |
|---|---|
| R40 | **One refresh, one time limit (F3).** A fresh read — every server call and every R38 round in it — gives up after **20 seconds** (`refreshDeadline`), and the screen shows its saved copy with "Saved copy from …" (or "Connect once …" when there is none). Each call keeps its own 15 s limit. 20 s is longer than one read normally takes on site signal, and short enough that nobody reaches for force-close. |
| R41 | **A late answer changes nothing (F3).** A read or a "which of my sends went through?" check that answers after the limit is thrown away: it writes no saved copy, marks nothing sent and changes nothing already shown. A payment it would have marked is settled by the background send, as before. |
| R42 | **A saved copy is only replaced by a later read (F3).** Its time is when the counted read **began** (not when it ended); `putCacheIfNewer` keeps the saved copy when an answer's read began earlier. So a slow earlier refresh can never overwrite what a quicker later one saved. |
| R43 | **My purchases and its My cash card are one refresh (F3).** `myPurchasesAndCash()` reads the list, the cash and the credits **at the same time**, inside **one** R38 read: one check before, one after, re-read when a payment went during it — so the list and the card count exactly the same sent payments, and both saved copies carry the same time and counts. If only the cash part fails, the list is still fresh and the card shows its saved copy (as in 0.8.0, where the card's failure never hid the list). My cash on its own also reads cash and credits at the same time. |
| R44 | **Only the latest load shows (F3).** On My purchases, My cash and a purchase's screen, a load that finishes after a newer one started changes nothing (a counter per screen). |
| R45 | **F1:** a voided purchase with nothing paid shows only its "Voided" tag — no payment tag. "Voided — paid, to recover" still shows (it says something new). |
| R46 | **F2:** when a purchase cannot be opened for want of signal, under the "No signal" notice: *"You can still record a payment from My purchases: tap "Record payment" on the purchase. It is saved on this phone and sent when there is signal."* (Tagalog underneath). Other failures show no such hint. |
| R47 | **A calendar-bound test is fixed.** `buyer_screens_test.dart` "ticked items" used a fixed "this week" cutoff of 24 Oct 2026 12:00 Manila while the buyer shell reads the real clock, so **four tests of the committed 0.8.0 fail from that moment on** (not a code fault: the app correctly moved on to next week). The group now uses a cutoff three days after whenever the test runs. |
| R49 | **The saved copy first, then the refresh (F3, revision 2).** On My purchases (the list and its My cash card) and on My cash, what the phone already has — the saved list and cash, with what is still on the phone — shows **at once** (`savedPurchasesAndCash()`, no server call), with "Saved copy from …", while the fresh refresh runs behind it and replaces it when it answers. Only until a refresh has answered: a later pull to refresh keeps what is shown until the new answer. Opening the app with no signal never shows an empty list for the 20 s. |
| R50 | **A purchase's screen is one load (F3, revision 2).** `purchaseScreen(id)` asks for the purchase and its payments **at the same time**, under **one** time limit for both (`refreshDeadline`, 20 s) — never one after the other. Whatever has not answered by then counts as no signal and its late answer is dropped; the payments on the phone show either way (with the payments read, or with the saved list's counts — R38). The latest-load check (R44) is kept. |
| R51 | **A balance and the payments on the phone always come from the same load (revision 3, R38).** On a purchase's screen the balance (`_pay`) and the payments still on the phone (`_queued`) are replaced together, from one load. If that load could not read the payments, the Payments part says "Payments show when there is signal." — no balance, no "left to pay", no Record payment — instead of keeping an earlier balance beside payments counted against the saved list. The purchase itself still shows. |
| R48 | **Launch ships 0.8.1+1010** (the user, 24 Oct: fix F1–F3 before launch, targeting 0.8.1). 0.8.0+1009 is never published; it stays on the test phone only until 0.8.1 replaces it. |
