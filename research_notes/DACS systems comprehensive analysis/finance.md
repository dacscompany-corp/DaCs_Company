# DACS Project Control and financial systems

## How do the financial workflows and business meanings fit together?

### Takeaway
Project Control is a project-cost and earned-profit system with funding-period ledgers, not a general ledger. Its budget, spending, invoicing, cash-collection, and planning concepts are distinct; several separate tracking modules intentionally never enter profit. The actual live functions substantiate most documented invariants, with important edge cases below. Research completed September 27, 2026 Asia/Manila, local repository only.

### Cited Findings
- **Project identity and lifecycle:** a Project Control job is a `folders` row, its `projects` rows are billing periods, and payroll/material transactions link to periods. The portal subscribes to these independent collections, maps them, and calculates cards rather than reading stored financial totals. `construction_projects` is a different system; its optional folder bridge does not make IDs interchangeable. [Architecture](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md); [subscriptions, js/portal-app.compiled.js:3528](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L3528); [card assembly, :610](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L610).
- **Financial values are not all in the obvious rows:** the current portal listens separately to `folderBudgets` and `projectBudgets`; expense-side subscriptions overlay period `monthlyBudget` from `_projectBudgetMap`. Review financial permission and migration behavior against these side tables, not just old folder/project schema. [js/portal-app.compiled.js:3605](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L3605); [js/expenses-module.js:704](../../js/expenses-module.js#L704).
- **Actual cost buckets:** payroll explicitly tagged direct/indirect/liability overrides role-name heuristics; legacy rows are classified by keywords. Indirect payroll and statutory burden marked `liabilityFor=indirect` go to Overhead. Direct burden stays Labor. Material equals sum of expense amounts. Project operating overhead excludes company scope and soft deletes. `Spent = Labor + Material + Overhead`, never plus Cover or planned reserve. [mapping, js/portal-app.compiled.js:226](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L226); [routing, :312](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L312); [buildProject, :403](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L403).
- **Earned versus forecast:** BOQ completion weights `qty*(materialRate+laborRate)` by each line's `percentCompletion`; rate overrides zero the affected component. Completion is clamped 0..1. No BOQ, zero total, or all-zero accomplished amount produces Forecast using the full contract. Company profit instead recognizes zero for unmeasured healthy jobs, and recognizes losses immediately if costs exceed contract. G&A is subtracted separately. This is an explicit application policy, not a claim of accounting-standard compliance. - **BOQ is a rich proposal/accomplishment document:** the save persists hierarchical costItems, discount, terms, client email, status and header; it separately synchronizes client email to the folder for access. It does not replace the folder contract budget in this save path. Multiple documents are possible; the portal currently chooses the document with the most top-level costItems, not latest updated/approved. - **Payroll and pakyaw:** create accepts daily calculation or lump sum, receipts, payment method, optional date, liability class and selected contracts. Multiple capped contracts split a payment pro-rata by remaining balance, rounding to cents and allocating final remainder to the last. Daily contracts are uncapped and selected alone. Each funding/contract leg becomes a payroll row. Cap overruns warn but are allowed after confirmation; caps are not hard payment constraints. - **Outsource and in-house labor:** one `labor_contracts` collection holds both, separated by category; both draw down through payroll. The picker intentionally offers both. - **Funding overflow:** new payroll takes the active fundable period, otherwise first fundable period, otherwise current period with unlimited fallback. Overflow from the chosen finite source uses a president period when available; otherwise it stays on the current period with a cover flag. Receipts appear on the first split and split metadata connects legs. ### Inferences
- Best conceptual separation: contract=agreed value; allocated=client funding envelope; Spent=actual recognized costs; Earned=accomplishment-based value; verified payments=cash receipts; reserve=plan; reimbursement/warranty/quotation=isolated tracking. Conflating any pair will cause a financial defect given the sourced flows above.
- The central pure allocation engine and shared report model are strong patterns worth extending to remaining duplicated money classification and export rules.

## What defects and risks deserve priority?

### Takeaway
The strongest verified finance findings are a pakyaw drawdown classification mismatch and dropped Cover disclosure. A separate Materials staff exposure affects both rendered amounts and export. These are source-confirmed behaviors and local function probes, not observed production incidents.

### Cited Findings
1. **High: indirect statutory burden can reduce pakyaw owed.** `_isOverheadPay` correctly categorizes `laborType=liability, liabilityFor=indirect` into Overhead, but `lcDrawsDown` only rejects `laborType=indirect`. Create/edit only clear contractId for indirect; picker hides only indirect and offers all folder contracts. Scenario: select capped contract C, choose liability and indirect burden, save 1,000. The row is overhead and still subtracts 1,000 from C's remaining balance. Source-extracted probe returns `{overhead:true,drawsPakyaw:true}`. Fix all create/edit/picker/drawdown predicates together, preserve legacy direct burden. [js/portal-app.compiled.js:312](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L312); [js/expenses-module.js:3109](../../js/expenses-module.js#L3109); [:4325](../../js/expenses-module.js#L4325); [:7152](../../js/expenses-module.js#L7152); [:8406](../../js/expenses-module.js#L8406); [:8592](../../js/expenses-module.js#L8592); [probe](finance-probes.cjs).
2. **Medium: payroll Cover flag is saved but lost in disclosure.** Scenario: client period has 1,000 remaining; save payroll 2,000; no president period exists. Overflow leg retains current period ID and sets coverExpense=true. Column exists on disk (`payroll.cover_expense`, 0001:134), shim registry directly maps payroll, so this is not a missing-column hypothesis. Mapper omits flag and coverCost tests only president IDs. Probe yields Spent 2,000, Cover 0 for a flagged payroll row. Reports also count only flagged expenses on nonpresident periods. Actual cost/profit remains counted; funding disclosure understates company advances. [js/expenses-module.js:3134](../../js/expenses-module.js#L3134); [:3164](../../js/expenses-module.js#L3164); [:4961](../../js/expenses-module.js#L4961); [js/portal-app.compiled.js:232](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L232); [:434](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L434); [supabase/migrations/0001_init.sql:134](../../supabase/migrations/0001_init.sql#L134); [js/supabase-config.js:93](../../js/supabase-config.js#L93); [probe](finance-probes.cjs).
3. **High confidentiality: Materials row/category peso amounts and CSV lack staff guard.** MaterialDrill mounts without owner restriction. Its KPI checks `_staff()`, but category peso total and transaction peso amount render unconditionally; staff check is oddly on percent-of-contract instead. CSV always has Amount(PHP) and button is unguarded. Platform researcher independently found table locations. Requires role-render/export test plus authenticated verification; no browser reproduction here. [js/portal-app.compiled.js:3226](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L3226); [:3249](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L3249); [:3253](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L3253); [:3197](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L3197); [:3237](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L3237); [:3940](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L3940).
4. **Reliability risk: payment verify and invoice generation are non-atomic and lack source-id idempotency in generator.** Payment status persists before invoice generation. Generator catches errors and returns null, leaving verified payment without invoice. It always adds a fresh invoice and allocates number from the local `_invoices` cache; two tabs/retries can race. No production duplicate is asserted. Recommend atomic server-side verify+invoice or resumable idempotent workflow with unique source key/server number generation and explicit pending-invoice state. [js/payment-requests.js:1009](../../js/payment-requests.js#L1009); [js/invoice-module.js:1099](../../js/invoice-module.js#L1099); [:1138](../../js/invoice-module.js#L1138); [:1164](../../js/invoice-module.js#L1164); [:2717](../../js/invoice-module.js#L2717).
5. **Semantic risk: Additional Works earned revenue uses parent completion.** Parent detail merges child contract into revenue, then sets completion from only parent BOQ. Example parent 1m at 50%, child 200k at 0% yields 600k earned rather than separately measured 500k (assuming independent accomplishment is intended). Folder grid cards calculate own folder only, while selected parent includes children, so scope differences may also confuse reconciliation. Need agreed rollup policy before changing. [js/portal-app.compiled.js:610](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L610); [:3752](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L3752); [:3770](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L3770).
6. **Data quality risk: BOQ selection is largest top-level item count, not revision/approval selection.** A more detailed old BOQ may win over a newer authoritative one. This is deterministic source behavior; whether affected folders actually contain competing documents is unverified. Recommend explicit authoritative document or documented unique-one-per-folder invariant. [js/portal-app.compiled.js:329](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L329).
7. **Export hardening risk:** Materials CSV and invoice CSV quote fields but do not neutralize formula-leading user text. Spreadsheet applications can interpret formula text despite CSV quoting. Use a shared neutralizing text-cell exporter and test across outputs; no exploit or opening of malicious CSV was performed. [js/portal-app.compiled.js:3199](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L3199); [js/invoice-module.js:2006](../../js/invoice-module.js#L2006).
8. **Low defensive risk:** buildProject sums all period budgets without excluding president, while report model forces cover budget zero. Current forms normally clear cover budgets, so this only matters for inconsistent/legacy data. Probe with a president period budget 1,000 yields allocated 1,000. Prefer invariant enforcement at aggregation boundary as well as forms. [js/portal-app.compiled.js:422](../../js/portal-app.compiled.js#L422); [js/expenses-module.js:4956](../../js/expenses-module.js#L4956); [:1091](../../js/expenses-module.js#L1091); [probe](finance-probes.cjs).

### Inferences
- Prioritize staff exposure and liability drawdown first, Cover disclosure second; next address verification/invoice retries, then clarify Additional Works accomplishment and BOQ authority. Do not alter isolation or earned-profit rules to solve these narrower issues.
- A passing source-level regression suite gives strong evidence of covered arithmetic, but misses combinations across classifiers/save paths and render branches. Add cross-surface scenarios rather than only unit arithmetic examples.

### Gaps
- These recommendations were not implemented. No data repair, schema write, deployment, production lookup, account login or client notification was performed.
- Do not classify invoice race, old-BOQ selection, or parent-child accomplishment as existing production losses without data and business-policy validation.
