# DACS operational systems and user journeys

## How do site operations and client journeys work?

### Takeaway
The repository contains two distinct operating ledgers plus a separate materials ordering system. Project Management (PM) is cost-plus site administration; Project Control (PC) is folder/billing-period costing. Attendance and receipt intake assist both but are not automatic accounting inputs. This is a static-source study conducted 26–27 September 2026, not verification of live production data or user sessions.

### Cited Findings
- **PM creation and ownership:** `pmSaveProject` saves client identity/email, partner email, optional PC folder link, project status, dates, estimate budget, and editable management fee (default 15%, clamped 0–100). Terms PDF upload is a separate step after the project write. Email is normalized. The two project IDs remain distinct; the optional `folderId` is explicit. Source: [js/pm-admin.js:1957](../../js/pm-admin.js#L1957), [1983](../../js/pm-admin.js#L1983), [2007](../../js/pm-admin.js#L2007).
- **Daily expense ledger despite weekly naming:** Daily Expense saves one `weeklyBills` document with date in `weekEndingDate`, `entries[]`, aggregated labor, materials, combined, overhead, directCostTotal, managementFee and grandTotal. Combined supply-and-install is already inside materials; overhead is a PM billable direct cost. Each entry can carry category, contract ID, split-payment metadata, and multiple receipt URLs. New rows are `Submitted`. Image failures intentionally do not abort the money save, but the user is warned. Source: [js/pm-admin.js:2900](../../js/pm-admin.js#L2900), [3600](../../js/pm-admin.js#L3600), [3635](../../js/pm-admin.js#L3635).
- **Labor/outsource caps:** PM labor contract paid-to-date is the sum of `entries` with matching contract ID and `type==='labor'`. Outsource uses the same contract table with a category discriminator and `both` daily entries. These are not PC `labor_contracts`/payroll. Source: [js/pm-admin.js:6128](../../js/pm-admin.js#L6128), [6159](../../js/pm-admin.js#L6159), [6174](../../js/pm-admin.js#L6174), [6184](../../js/pm-admin.js#L6184).
- **PM procurement does not itself book a daily expense:** Client accepts purchasing responsibility (`Assigned to Client`) or hands it to admin (`Assigned to Admin`); purchase completion stores `Bought by Client`/`Bought by Company`, amount, receipt, notes and timestamps on procurementList. The inspected handlers do not create daily bills, invoices, or inventory movements. Encoding a purchase in costs is therefore a separate operational action. Source: [js/client-management-app.js:2328](../../js/client-management-app.js#L2328), [2435](../../js/client-management-app.js#L2435), [2465](../../js/client-management-app.js#L2465), [js/pm-admin.js:3939](../../js/pm-admin.js#L3939).
- **Progress is not one universal number:** PM overview uses completed milestone percentages when any are configured, otherwise completed/total milestone count, clamped 0–100. PM accomplishment reports have their own item/group pricing and weighted accomplishment formulas and are stored under `accomplishmentReports`. Neither automatically supplies PC BOQ earned revenue. Source: [js/pm-admin.js:187](../../js/pm-admin.js#L187), [4239](../../js/pm-admin.js#L4239), [4499](../../js/pm-admin.js#L4499). PC distinction: [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §3,5,6](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).
- **Weekly fund collection:** Admin now records a fund request per Sunday-start week, amount, notes and received flag. Amount-versus-daily-cost and a running balance are computed. It is a collection checklist, not an additional expense source. The net/running balance uses all requested amounts, not only received funds; expenditure is summed only for weeks represented by requests. Source: [js/pm-admin.js:5525](../../js/pm-admin.js#L5525), [5562](../../js/pm-admin.js#L5562), [5578](../../js/pm-admin.js#L5578), [5598](../../js/pm-admin.js#L5598), [5673](../../js/pm-admin.js#L5673).
- **Design client journey:** `client-app.js` subscribes to BOQs by normalized client email, then loads related folders and folder budgets, billing periods and period budgets. President-funded cover periods are excluded from the client allocation list. Notifications and shared client payment code are separate paths. Source: [js/client-app.js:263](../../js/client-app.js#L263), [288](../../js/client-app.js#L288), [303](../../js/client-app.js#L303), [1090](../../js/client-app.js#L1090), [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §1](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).
- **Construction client/partner journey:** Shared `client-management-app.js` checks that the profile role matches the requested portal. Projects are selected by clientEmail; partners merge partnerEmail and legacy clientEmail matches, deduplicate and get a multi-project picker. Project details load bills, payment requests, funds, logs, milestones, reports, and procurement. Partner display intentionally hides fee/payment surfaces and billing folder; both HTML pages must stay aligned. Source: [js/client-management-app.js:85](../../js/client-management-app.js#L85), [233](../../js/client-management-app.js#L233), [289](../../js/client-management-app.js#L289), [3383](../../js/client-management-app.js#L3383), [3819](../../js/client-management-app.js#L3819), [4454](../../js/client-management-app.js#L4454).
- **Agreements:** Construction clients sign Cost-Plus terms once; partners sign a separately tracked Partnership agreement once. Current routing applies project-specific terms to clients only, when attached, even though old function/table names still say partner. Signed-version rendering and audit event paths retain historical document context. Source: [js/client-management-app.js:4515](../../js/client-management-app.js#L4515), [562](../../js/client-management-app.js#L562), [4869](../../js/client-management-app.js#L4869), [4973](../../js/client-management-app.js#L4973). The agreement alignment document is historical analysis, not authoritative current flow: its one-project claim conflicts with the current picker [docs/partnership-agreement-system-alignment.md](../../docs/partnership-agreement-system-alignment.md).
- **Closeout:** Owner chooses completed or terminated. Final amount uses accumulated actual costs plus billed fees, less payment receipts; project estimate is not treated as a fixed contract. An approved closeout record is created, project status is updated, final invoice attempted for positive balance, client notified; completion additionally calls the isolated warranty reserve module. Source: [js/termination-requests.js:359](../../js/termination-requests.js#L359), [702](../../js/termination-requests.js#L702), [752](../../js/termination-requests.js#L752), [804](../../js/termination-requests.js#L804).
- **Separate Construction ordering:** `construction-module.js` remains reachable through Construction → Materials & Orders / `consBatch`, with batches, urgent requests, inventory, history and delivery status. This is distinct from PM procurementList. Item delivery triggers inventory stock additions by matching item name. Source: [js/admin.js:2254](../../js/admin.js#L2254), [admin.html:6350](../../admin.html#L6350), [js/construction-module.js:328](../../js/construction-module.js#L328), [407](../../js/construction-module.js#L407).
- **Public lead intake and reputation:** Appointment form directly creates a pending appointment with contact/service/message fields, then promises contact within 24 hours. Admin has list/status/export paths. Feedback automatically marks 4–5 stars approved, 1–3 pending; public display further filters approved ratings >=4, newest six. These are implemented product choices, not proof of an actual 24-hour service process. Source: [js/script.js:97](../../js/script.js#L97), [124](../../js/script.js#L124), [351](../../js/script.js#L351), [440](../../js/script.js#L440), [js/admin.js:1014](../../js/admin.js#L1014), [1152](../../js/admin.js#L1152).

### Inferences
- Product documentation should explain three separate meanings: PC costing, PM cost-plus administration, and Construction material ordering; otherwise users can assume procurement purchases or stock movements already count in job costs. Evidence: separate writes above.
- Milestone completion, BOQ accomplishment, and schedule elapsed progress should be distinctly labelled; they answer different questions. Evidence: separate milestone and accomplishment formula paths above.

### Gaps
- No logged-in browser sessions, live tables, production balances, deployed RPC versions, or account permissions exercised. Source claims are code behavior, not deployment assertions.
- Site logs and walkthroughs have client readers and shim registry entries, but a repository-wide JS/HTML search found no authoring handler; they may be legacy or externally populated. Readers: [js/client-management-app.js:369](../../js/client-management-app.js#L369), [925](../../js/client-management-app.js#L925); storage: [js/supabase-config.js:168](../../js/supabase-config.js#L168). Do not describe them as complete end-to-end workflows.
- Native attendance worker app resides outside this repository; dormant `android/` client TWA status is documented rather than independently observed [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §1,4](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

## How do attendance, receipts, notifications and outputs connect?

### Takeaway
Attendance and the receipt inbox are intentionally support systems. Attendance supplies presence/evidence and a separately tracked bonus, not labor costing. Receipt capture supplies documentation awaiting manual encoding, not a second ledger.

### Cited Findings
- **Attendance admin:** Today LEFT-JOINs active worker/teamLeader profiles with a day's records so missing records remain visible. Worker roster, worker creation/editing, photos, project selection, reports, forgotten-timeout resolution and weekly rewards are implemented. Worker creation uses shared server-side account creation then a position update; deactivated roster entries remain manageable. Source: [js/attendance-admin.js:658](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L658), [2218](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L2218), [2369](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L2369), [2533](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L2533).
- **Data boundary:** Attendance writes use RPCs, time-in work date is PH-local, and time-out derives elapsed minutes. Later migrations add real PC/PM project pickers, schedule exceptions, effective-dated geofences, offline/location verdicts, and hidden-project configuration. Admin reports expose location/clock discrepancies rather than treating every image as verified. Source: [supabase/migrations/0050_attendance.sql:22](../../supabase/migrations/0050_attendance.sql#L22), [387](../../supabase/migrations/0050_attendance.sql#L387), [542](../../supabase/migrations/0050_attendance.sql#L542), [0068_attendance_geofence.sql:16](../../supabase/migrations/0068_attendance_geofence.sql#L16), [js/attendance-admin.js:672](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L672), [2720](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L2720), [3873](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L3873).
- **Forgotten checkout:** Admin calls `attendance_abandon` rather than fabricating timeout/hours. It checks prior dates/open records and carries audit metadata. Source: [js/attendance-admin.js:326](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L326), [2162](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L2162), [supabase/migrations/0061_attendance_abandon.sql](../../supabase/migrations/0061_attendance_abandon.sql).
- **Weekly bonus:** Monday–Friday reward weeks differ from PM Sunday-start fund weeks. Future dates are pending, not absences. Live results are forecasts and cannot be downloaded; frozen records can be marked handed over without accounting side effects. Evaluation is triggered by the page, scans eight weeks back, not pg_cron. Staff now see no bonus amounts or CSV button. Source: [js/attendance-admin.js:408](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L408), [417](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L417), [3950](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L3950), [4100](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L4100), [4118](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L4118), [4228](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L4228), [4304](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L4304).
- **Receipt pipeline:** PWA POST share target is intercepted by the service worker, copied into temporary Cache API entries, then redirected to share-capture. Wizard tags PC/PM, project, entry type and optional category/labor name, uploads each file, creates a pending expenseInbox row per file, then clears cache. It stores no numeric amounts. Encoding status in `expense-inbox.js` is a manual checklist and does not write expense/payroll/weeklyBill. Source: [service-worker.js:38](../../service-worker.js#L38), [116](../../service-worker.js#L116), [share-capture.html:438](../../share-capture.html#L438), [js/expense-inbox.js:18](../../js/expense-inbox.js#L18), [369](../../js/expense-inbox.js#L369).
- **PWA scope:** Service worker explicitly has no general offline app caching; it handles push and receipt share intake. Do not promise the back office works fully offline. Source: [service-worker.js:1](../../service-worker.js#L1), [142](../../service-worker.js#L142).
- **Notifications:** Client change events may fan out into owner and staff personal notification collections; client portals subscribe to their own inbox, update read state, and expose push subscription flows. Notification success is separate from the underlying business save. Source: [js/client-management-app.js:11](../../js/client-management-app.js#L11), [1023](../../js/client-management-app.js#L1023), [3280](../../js/client-management-app.js#L3280), [js/termination-requests.js:845](../../js/termination-requests.js#L845).
- **Output fanout to preserve:** PM overview report and data view print (`pmOvPrintReport`, `pmDvPrint`), category SOA and labor/outsource statements, all contracts print, accomplishment report browser sheet and real PDF, client weekly report, single bill sheet, agreement signed PDF and admin historical reprint, attendance daily/worker CSV and reward CSV, admin appointment reports. PM report print has its own staff amount guard. Sources: [js/pm-admin.js:1046](../../js/pm-admin.js#L1046), [1501](../../js/pm-admin.js#L1501), [3401](../../js/pm-admin.js#L3401), [7627](../../js/pm-admin.js#L7627), [8064](../../js/pm-admin.js#L8064), [8545](../../js/pm-admin.js#L8545), [js/pm-report-print.js:54](../../js/pm-report-print.js#L54), [284](../../js/pm-report-print.js#L284), [314](../../js/pm-report-print.js#L314), [js/client-management-app.js:2541](../../js/client-management-app.js#L2541), [4973](../../js/client-management-app.js#L4973), [js/attendance-admin.js:3398](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L3398).

### Inferences
- Staff hiding must be checked independently in every screen, file export and print, since the code has role-specific safeguards on some outputs but no universal formatter guard.
- Operational report totals should share pure helpers, with fixtures that span different expense types, fund weeks, recipients and statuses, to prevent drift between browser and exports.

### Gaps
- Web push delivery, mobile share integration, upload signing and native offline synchronization were not exercised. Server-side push/security review belongs to the parallel platform researcher.
- No forensic comparison of every mirrored HTML element; both are confirmed live required surfaces, but template parity is not certified.

## Which issues and risks warrant action first?

### Takeaway
The strongest confirmed issues are omitted PM overhead during fee changes, incomplete staff money hiding in PM, and closeout success messaging that can outpace completed side effects. Other findings concern retries, partial writes and stale documentation, with impact dependent on runtime conditions.

### Cited Findings
- **P1 — Confirmed formula defect: changing PM fee drops overhead from updated grand total.** `_pmRecomputeUnpaidBills` computes direct as labor + materials only and rewrites fee/grand, while `_pmWeekTotals` uses labor + materials + overhead. Example: labor 100, materials 200, overhead 50, new fee 10% produces 330 instead of 385; persisted directCostTotal stays 350. This can make saved grand total disagree with overview/closeout. Fix recomputation via one authoritative direct-cost helper and add fixtures for overhead-only/mixed bills and paid/partial exclusions. Sources: [js/pm-admin.js:2052](../../js/pm-admin.js#L2052), [2060](../../js/pm-admin.js#L2060), [2900](../../js/pm-admin.js#L2900).
- **P1 — Confirmed source-level staff money visibility inconsistency:** Staff retain PM Projects/Workspace navigation (only Warranty is removed), and Daily Expense render writes peso totals with `_pmPeso`; revolving fund renders `_fmt` amounts. `_fmt` has no role guard. By contrast accomplishment print and attendance explicitly hide amounts. These reachable code paths violate the stated staff rule unless an unobserved external layer hides the whole content; no such PM CSS rule found in inspected CSS search. Verify logged-in staff and audit all PM forms/prints rather than claiming a runtime exploit. Sources: [js/admin.js:2328](../../js/admin.js#L2328), [js/pm-admin.js:2918](../../js/pm-admin.js#L2918), [5625](../../js/pm-admin.js#L5625), [6116](../../js/pm-admin.js#L6116).
- **P1/P2 — Confirmed misleading closeout success path:** `_applyCloseout` catches project-status and invoice creation errors; `trConfirmCloseout` still displays “Project completed/terminated” and “Final invoice ... issued” based solely on positive balance. This is a code-proven possible false success, not evidence a real closeout failed. Make effects observable/idempotent with explicit pending/failed stages and accurate result text; consider a server transaction for core status/record changes. Sources: [js/termination-requests.js:779](../../js/termination-requests.js#L779), [808](../../js/termination-requests.js#L808), [836](../../js/termination-requests.js#L836).
- **P2 — Reachable separate Construction inventory race/retry risk:** UI shows Delivered action only for Purchased items and replaces it with an icon once refreshed, so ordinary repeated clicking after refresh is not offered. But delivery mutation has no previous-status check/idempotency key; it unconditionally invokes stock increment after any delivered action, without disabling in-flight action. Two tabs/concurrent submissions or stale UI can therefore double-add, and stock updates are read/modify/write (lost updates possible). Request update and stock change are separate, unawaited operations. This belongs to the separately reachable Construction module, not PM procurement. Sources: [js/construction-module.js:282](../../js/construction-module.js#L282), [328](../../js/construction-module.js#L328), [390](../../js/construction-module.js#L390), [407](../../js/construction-module.js#L407), [js/admin.js:2254](../../js/admin.js#L2254). Recommend a transactional delivery event with unique request/item identity and unit-aware stock identity.
- **P2 — Partial multi-photo submission retry can duplicate receipts:** Each photo uploads/adds independently; if later photo fails, already saved photos remain, cache clears only at end, and Send retry iterates all photos again. No per-file completion key is persisted in the inspected handler. Recommend idempotent intake identifiers/per-file completion and resumable status. Source: [share-capture.html:443](../../share-capture.html#L443).
- **P2 — Agreement gate fails open on read errors:** Project-specific terms helper explicitly returns true on failures and on no user, and routing defaults accepted=true on error. This is confirmed UI behavior, but whether underlying data is protected despite it depends on current RLS (platform researcher to assess). Source: [js/client-management-app.js:570](../../js/client-management-app.js#L570), [5054](../../js/client-management-app.js#L5054). Recommend an error/retry state if this gate is a required business condition.
- **P2 — Revolving-fund semantics drift:** Current admin fund collection checklist uses requests and daily expenses, whereas `cmRenderRevolving` still reads legacy fundAmount/totalSpent/currentBalance plus revolvingFundExpenses and calls it petty cash replenished Fridays. Weekly client paths also load the new requests. Distinct data sets can make screens legitimately differ yet appear contradictory. Clarify/reconcile labels and authoritative sources; do not simply add both into cost totals. Sources: [js/pm-admin.js:5525](../../js/pm-admin.js#L5525), [js/client-management-app.js:343](../../js/client-management-app.js#L343), [354](../../js/client-management-app.js#L354), [3637](../../js/client-management-app.js#L3637).
- **P2 — Bonus evaluation error handling:** `attRunDueEvaluations` awaits `sbClient.rpc` without examining returned `{error}` and then appends week to frozen list. Other attendance RPC paths correctly inspect the response; ordinary Supabase error results can therefore be treated as success. No production occurrence verified. It also evaluates only a trailing eight-week window and depends on opening this screen. Source: [js/attendance-admin.js:3961](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L3961), contrast [2166](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L2166). Recommend checking returned errors and a visible catch-up status.
- **P3 — Documentation correction required:** ARCHITECTURE says `weekly_bills.entries/combined/direct_cost_total` remain uncaptured drift, but migration 0020 now explicitly adds all three. It also says attendance rewards are staff-visible/undecided, whereas code hides amounts and CSV. Do not repeat these stale assertions in the final report. Source: [supabase/migrations/0020_schema_drift_catchup.sql:53](../../supabase/migrations/0020_schema_drift_catchup.sql#L53), [js/attendance-admin.js:4100](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L4100), [4118](../../js/attendance-admin.js#L4118), compared with [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md §4,8](../../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).
- **Deletion nuance:** PM manual cascade lists only selected child collections, but original SQL FKs cascade daily logs, walkthroughs, revolving funds and partner agreements. Missing manual enumeration alone is NOT evidence of orphaned rows. External storage and non-FK documents are separate cleanup questions. Sources: [js/pm-admin.js:2078](../../js/pm-admin.js#L2078), [supabase/migrations/0001_init.sql:332](../../supabase/migrations/0001_init.sql#L332), [368](../../supabase/migrations/0001_init.sql#L368), [399](../../supabase/migrations/0001_init.sql#L399), [0018_partner_agreements.sql:18](../../supabase/migrations/0018_partner_agreements.sql#L18).

### Inferences
- Sequence recommended: (1) PM fee/overhead regression test and repair; (2) logged-in staff PM audit across render/form/print; (3) closeout recovery and accurate status; (4) idempotent delivery and receipt intake; (5) unify revolving-fund semantics and clean documentation. This ordering prioritizes money correctness and role exposure over maintainability.
- Add workflow tests across save → reload → client render → print/export, including overhead and combined entries, fee edits, missing receipts, partial payments, completed jobs, and restricted roles. Existing pure test coverage does not certify runtime multi-step workflows.
